# OpsBlaze

## Platform

Web. The Community workbench runs on a trusted local simulator with PostgreSQL, Temporal and a same-origin API. Traditional desktop work is primary. Smaller screens support the same actions and evidence through reflow.

## People and tasks

Operators admit an incident, follow its investigation, refresh durable state and hand off uncertain cases. Reviewers inspect an exact proposed simulator action and approve, reject or revoke within the rules already enforced by the API. Security administrators alone can authorize eligible experience redrive. A read-only user can inspect records permitted by the API.

An incident begins with a tenant, target and idempotency key. Investigation collects evidence and assessments within its probe budget. Evidence and receipts are distinct from an independently witnessed outcome. Review belongs with its exact plan digest, version and expiry. Operators need connection or request failures to remain visible during an incident.

## Workspaces

- **Incidents** keeps the recent register, intake, current snapshot, investigation, review and durable event history together.
- **Evidence and trace** shows the selected incident's evidence records, provenance, causal links, source inspection and effect receipts.
- **Queue health** shows tenant-scoped counts and age from the API.
- **Experience review** shows dead-letter records and authorized review or redrive.
- **Replay lab** runs existing deterministic paired experiments with their real fixture, seed, oracle, read cost and virtual time.

Local credentials remain in browser memory. OIDC settings and routes retain the existing trust model. External recovery, independent Enterprise qualification and live policy promotion remain outside this console's supported operation.
